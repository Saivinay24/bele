# 02. What exists in India, and the gap each leaves

| System | Reality, with numbers | Gap a farmer's agent can fill |
|---|---|---|
| **e-NAM** | 1.80 crore farmers, 2.73 lakh traders and 4,724 FPOs registered by March 2026 ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/apr/doc2026413845801.pdf)). FY25 turnover Rs 80,262 cr; intra-state inter-mandi trade Rs 1,769 cr (about 2.2%); inter-state only Rs 21 cr, down 50% ([Financial Express via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/ja/stories/newspaper/Financial-Express-Hyderabad/ENAM-REMAINS-LOCAL-INTERSTATE-TRADE-MINIMAL)). Registered vs active: Jalgaon 37,903 registered, 1,170 traded (3%) ([Lok Sabha AU1504](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2998079/1/AU1504_HVk1tf.pdf)) | e-NAM works as a local mandi screen, not a market across mandis. The farmer still needs someone to find remote buyers, move the goods and make sure he is paid |
| **Agmarknet** | Daily price and arrivals per mandi and commodity; open data on data.gov.in. Sparse and irregular for small mandis | Raw feed: no take-home price, no uncertainty, no action |
| **Bharat-VISTAAR** | Launched 17 Feb 2026; Rs 150 cr in Budget 2026-27; helpline 155261 and AI assistant "Bharati"; uses AgriStack, IMD and mandi prices; Hindi and English first ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/shivraj-chouhan-launches-ai-powered-bharat-vistaar-platform-for-farmers/), [Zee Business](https://www.zeebiz.com/india/news-what-is-bharat-vistaar-ai-tool-that-helps-farmers-get-crop-advice-through-a-simple-phone-call-explained-390612)) | Advisory: it answers questions but does not sell, store or settle debt. Bele is designed as an action layer that can sit alongside it |
| **ONDC agri** | 9,000+ FPOs on ONDC by July 2025, mostly packaged goods ([ONDC](https://ondc.org/blog/farmer-groups-expand-business-outreach-via-ondc)) | Raw harvest lots from individual farmers are not its main use case; usable as a source of buyers through FPOs |
| **Buyer-side aggregators** (Ninjacart, DeHaat and similar) | Procure for retail and institutions, or bundle inputs and advisory with buying ([AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/deep-tech/agri-e-commerce-is-ripe-for-the-taking)) | Each is one buyer with its own price. A farmer-side agent invites them as bidders alongside the mandi trader |
| **Warehouse-receipt lenders** (Arya.ag and similar) | About Rs 2,000 cr commodity finance in FY25 plus over Rs 10,000 cr of bank lending on its receipts ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/arya-ag-nbfc-arm-does-rs-2-000-crore-commodity-financing-in-fy25-125033100806_1.html)); rates 12.5 to 12.8% vs 24 to 36% from commission agents (company-sourced, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3079406)) | Serves FPOs and larger lots best. A small farmer still needs consolidation, paperwork help and someone to clear the trader first |
| **WDRA eNWR and e-Kisan Upaj Nidhi** | Pledge finance on electronic warehouse receipts: Rs 4,271.61 cr in FY25 ([WDRA Annual Report 2024-25](https://wdra.gov.in/documents/d/wdra/wdra_englsih-an_24-25-final-to-print-compressed)); cumulative over Rs 10,000 cr by Feb 2025 ([Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/xhub/featured-insights/national-e-repository-ltd-crosses-10000-crore-in-pledge-finance-against-enwr)). Credit guarantee scheme with a Rs 1,000 cr corpus launched Dec 2024. KCC holders can get 7% a year for 6 months under interest subvention (secondary, [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/agriculture/policies-and-schemes/crops-related/e-kisan-upaj-nidhi)). The Finance Ministry pushed banks to lend more in April 2025 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/agriculture/finmin-pushes-banks-to-boost-farm-loans-against-e-warehouse-receipts-125041800847_1.html)) | The money and the guarantee exist. Uptake is held back by discovery, paperwork, minimum lot sizes and the farmer's existing debt to the trader. What is missing is orchestration |

## Summary

- The buyers are already on e-NAM, but trade stays local.
- The cheap credit already exists, but small farmers do not reach it.
- The advice already exists, but nothing acts on it.

No existing system resolves the trader's claim on the harvest, which is what decides where the farmer sells. Bele adds that resolution and ties the existing pieces together.
